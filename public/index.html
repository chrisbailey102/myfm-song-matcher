Spotify Import

Paste a playlist URL or pick from your library. Spotify only returns tracks for playlists you own or collaborate on. Adds to My Collection and Entire Library.

Paste track list

Paste ARTIST - TITLE lines (one per line). Creates a Spotify playlist and imports it into Song Matcher. If create fails with a permission error, use Reconnect Spotify once and allow playlist editing on the consent screen.

Custom playlist

Create an empty playlist, then drag tracks from Entire Library or other playlists to copy them in. Right-click a track (or Delete / Backspace) to remove it.

Excel Upload

Upload a spreadsheet to enrich BPM/key and download a CSV. Does not add tracks to My Collection.
